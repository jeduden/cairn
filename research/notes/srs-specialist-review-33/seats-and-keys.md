The concepts are: seat (seat kind, add), run seat, device seat, room id, seat id, seat key, device key (device certificate, device scope), principal key (service-account certificate), token key (token certificate), seat certificate (key set), access token, authenticator and CI key.

- I10 reads the same in invariants.md, my "key set" entry, ADM-08 and CON-05.

- The hub relations that use my concepts agree with my file, except the one in finding 12.

### Findings (summary; details in the JSON)

1. **LANE-23 seat id.** It derives the seat id from "that seat key", meaning the key the seat joins with. A seat that rotated its key and is then added again would get a different id, which contradicts the same requirement and SEC-27 ("keep its seat id"). The model derives the id from the seat's first key.
2. **LANE-23 certificate chain.** It requires a join's seat certificate to chain to the principal key, with no "once PRV-10 ships". LANE-01 (M1) already has device seats join before PRV-10, when no principal key certifies a device key.
3. **"Signed with that device seat".** SEC-32, 09a and two scenarios say a seat signs, where its seat key does. This blurs a room act with a device-key-signed principal act. §1.4 has the correct wording.
4. **Boundary register rows 19 and 27.** They give only device keys as encryption targets and drop "or a token-key-only node's token key" (PEER-08, PEER-12). That understates reach in the register.
5. **RCL-11 scenario.** It names a seat "bob", a name every other scenario uses for a principal.
6. **`room_bar`.** Its parameter `key` selects a principal key but is not named for that concept.
7. **Question: rotation.** Does a rotated seat key, or a rotated device key, get a new certificate? No requirement says so. Without one, the new key may not chain, so a LANE-25 bar may not cover it (T26), and SEC-25 or PEER-07 may refuse its segments.
8. **Question: paired phone reading.** My file's device scope covers only acts, posts and pins. LANE-16, PRV-10, OWN-16 and the Role entry also limit a paired phone's reading by its device scope.
9. **Question, my file: access token.** It says an access token is minted to "carry an invite link". LANE-18 and git-and-forge's Qualified links have the invite link carrying the access token.
10. **Question, my file: token key citation.** The token key's limits are cited to PRV-10; they are stated in PEER-06.
11. **Question: PIN-11.** Its reason "seat certificate or token key does not cover the pin's room" looks impossible. A pin's room is its author seat's room, the seat certificate is scoped to that room, and the result is the same on every node. The pins-and-context specialist shares this one.
12. **Hub (domain-model agent).** "a new one for each seat key minted anew" also reads as covering rotation, which keeps the seat.

None of the fixes needs new invariant wording.
