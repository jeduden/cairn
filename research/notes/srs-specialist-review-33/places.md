### Needs fixing (SRS and scenarios)

1. **LANE-15 treats every foreign room as one read from a bundle.** In `/home/user/cairn/docs/srs/05b-lane-requirements.md:27`, every foreign room must state that its content "is asserted by the bundle's principal key". The model's Foreign room also covers a room every seat of the principal has left or lost to a kick or a bar. PIN-10's and RCL-10's scenarios ("L5", "left-room") and the hub rely on that. Such a room has no bundle, so the rule cannot be met. Fix: scope the bundle statements to "one read from a bundle".
2. **T29 cites the wrong requirement for the sandbox.** `/home/user/cairn/docs/srs/06-security.md:70` says "a sandbox (SEC-19)". SEC-19 is the boundary register; the sandbox requirement is OWN-22, which T21 already cites. Fix: "(OWN-22)".
3. **OWN-11 uses "device" for the authenticator.** `/home/user/cairn/docs/srs/05c-principal-and-peer-requirements.md:27` says "certified to enforce user verification on the device". In the model, Device means a node or a paired phone. Read that way, an authenticator could rely on the host's own user verification, which weakens the rule against T21's software authenticator. Fix: "on the authenticator itself".
4. **Two scenarios say a room is "shared" with nodes (minor).** `/home/user/cairn/features/security.feature:371` and `/home/user/cairn/features/peer.feature:145` do this. Visibility's "shared" means shared with the room's principals; the hub's verb for nodes is "holds".
5. **"Foreign history" (minor).** It appears at `/home/user/cairn/docs/srs/02-context.md:20` and `:99`, and in `/home/user/cairn/.claude/agents/persona-oss-maintainer.md:40`, which the §2.5 gate keeps in step. "Foreign" is defined only for rooms; fix: "a foreign room's history".

### Questions for the stakeholder (gaps in `places.md`)

6. **Is a cross-room post's sending room a foreign room?** Suppose this node holds the sending seat's writer only to show the post, and its principal has no seat in the sending room. By the model's definition that room is then foreign. RCL-05 says the `room` scope covers the cross-room posts a room shows, and also that a foreign room is never reached through `room` or `rooms`. Separately, LANE-29 lets a seat pull the post without a seat in the sending room, while PEER-02 serves segments only to principals with a seat. PEER-02 is marked OQ-41, but OQ-41's text covers only principal acts, not cross-room posts.
7. **What do "private" and "shared" visibility constrain?** Only publishing and blind-peer enrollment check visibility. Nothing says whether an invite, admission change or join into a non-personal private room is refused, or makes it shared. PEER-02 serves, and PEER-08's git carrier stores, segments whatever the visibility. So a room can show "private" while other principals hold it.
8. **`places.md` uses "shares" for a node clone's personal room** (lines 23 and 41–42). This clashes with Visibility's "shared". The hub's verb "holds" would fit.
9. **PEER-05 says "A node marked ephemeral"**, but neither the model nor any requirement defines that mark. The model's "ephemeral node" (`places.md:17`) is only a description.
