# 5. Functional requirements: room

The room family of §5, kept in its own file. Each table lists:
**ID** · **Pri** · **Requirement** · **Ver** (verification method) ·
**Traces** (invariants).

## 5.11 Room (LANE)

LANE holds what a room is and what Cairn derives about it: identity,
authors, results, evidence, landing, membership and roles. Room state
derives from room acts, principal acts and expire acts alone
(LANE-31).
