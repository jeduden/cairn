# 5. Functional requirements: room

The room family of §5, kept in its own file. Each table lists:
**ID** · **Pri** · **Requirement** · **Ver** (verification method) ·
**Traces** (invariants).

## 5.11 Room (LANE)

LANE holds what a room is and what Cairn derives about it: identity,
actors, results, evidence, landing, membership and roles. A room is
where an intent is worked on: its intent, conversation, seats
and pins, and its branches with their worktrees, edits, runs and
results, one attempt per branch.
