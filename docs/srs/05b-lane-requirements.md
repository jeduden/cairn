# 5. Functional requirements: lane and lane view

The lane families of §5, kept in their own file. Each table lists:
**ID** · **Pri** · **Requirement** · **Ver** (verification method) ·
**Traces** (invariants).

## 5.11 Lane (LANE)

LANE holds what a lane is and what Cairn derives about it: identity,
actors, results, evidence, landing and membership. A lane is a branch
with its worktrees, the sessions and people working on it, and their
conversations, edits, runs and results.

## 5.12 Lane view (VIEW)

VIEW holds what a person reads, on every surface: the browser lane
view (`cairn-ui`), the TUI, the CLI and the phone. VIEW has no write
path; every act a person takes is in OWN. The priorities of rows that
open the lane view to v1 are provisional until OQ-30 settles the v1
scope.
