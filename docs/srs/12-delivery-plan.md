## 12.2 Milestones

Existing milestone ids stay stable; M7 to M9 follow M6. Execution order
puts experience before sync: M1, M2, M3, M7, M4, M5, M8, M9. The
ENG-29 review gates M7 and M8. M7's priorities are provisional until
OQ-30 settles the v1 scope.

Rows in M1 to M3 need widening acts before M7: ADM-04 (changed) and
SEC-12 in M1, PIN-10 in M2, and ADM-06's backup restore in M3. M1 therefore
delivers the minimal part of OWN-11 and OWN-12: the three principal-act
classes (cut, neutral, widening), and widening acts made only through
the CLI at a terminal, with OWN-22's precondition applied (the run's
sandbox state and a risk acceptance made there). Remote principal
surfaces (OWN-02) and presence proofs stay in M7, which completes OWN-11
and OWN-12.
