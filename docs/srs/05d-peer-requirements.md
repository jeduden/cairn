# 5. Functional requirements: peer network

## 5.14 Peer network (PEER)

Being a peer never makes content trusted: what a peer brings in is
trusted only as PRV-02 classifies it.

| PEER-01 | P2  | Sync MUST run only in the peer component (B2), off by default. Turning it on is a widening principal act the CLI records; its own entry point, started by the person, a service manager or an ephemeral node's entrypoint, never by a core process, MUST run only while that act stands. On an ephemeral node, the principal's write-once environment variable MUST stand in for that act, minting the node's access token being the widening act; the environment variable MUST be recorded as a structural event, not a principal act, and audited. Whether it ships in the core's executable or its own MUST NOT change its boundary. With it absent or stopped, every other requirement MUST be met as with no peer component.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | T    | I4, I9  |
| PEER-02 | P2  | Every peer MUST be able to hold complete copies of the rooms it shares and serve each room's segments to the enrolled peers whose principal has a seat in that room (LANE-16) and to the blind peers that hold the room (PEER-12), and to no other (open: OQ-41). No peer MAY be required for two others to exchange segments, and no third-party service for enrollment, discovery or relay (CON-06).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | T, D | I4      |
| PEER-03 | P2  | Peers MUST exchange sealed ranges of writers: closed segments and the sealed prefix of an open one. Two peers holding the same segments MUST derive byte-identical room state and index, whatever order or partition they arrived through (ADM-08). A node cut off from every peer MUST keep appending to its own writers without slowing its agents.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | T    | I9, I10 |
| PEER-04 | P2  | An event written on any connected peer MUST appear in every connected peer's room view within 5 s† of being written, across at most one relay hop through an enrolled peer, carried as part of a sealed range (REC-19).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A    | I9      |
| PEER-05 | P2  | A node marked ephemeral MUST seal and offer its open segment to a connected peer at every `Stop`, `SubagentStop` and `SessionEnd`, and at least every 30 s† while a run is active. A connected ephemeral node MUST offer each sealed range as soon as it is sealed. A writer MUST be marked retired by a principal act or by the expire act ending the access token whose token key certified its seat key; once its clock passes that access token's expiry, the node that minted it MUST record that expire act, or, before PRV-10 ships, refuse every later use of the access token. Either act MUST name the writer's last accepted `seq`, and no derivation reads a clock. A peer MUST still accept its segments past that `seq` that continue its chain without a fork, marked delivered after retirement, and only a revocation refuses them. A retired writer's lost tail, or that of a writer a new seat replaced (REC-24, ADM-06), MUST be shown as a missing range after the last `seq` received, never as a quiet end or as `behind`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | T    | I1, I6  |
| PEER-06 | P2  | Enrollment MUST verify the key on both nodes, by matching words or a scanned code; discovery MUST NOT enroll. An access token for certifying an ephemeral node's seats MUST be scoped to rooms, to creating new rooms or continuing named ones, and to an expiry. It MUST carry the bound identity of each repository the node works in (LANE-02), a token key, certified by the issuing node's device key and limited to those rooms, the node's own personal room and the expiry, with which the node certifies its own seat keys without reaching any peer, and every enrolled peer network address it may deliver to. A token key signs no principal act, so every pin the node writes MUST restore only once a principal stamps it from one of its devices whose device scope allows it (PRV-10), and no trust grant covers its posts or pins (OWN-29). An access token that names a room to continue MUST carry that room's qualifying pins (PIN-10) as signed events keeping their provenance (`operator` for a device seat's pin, `assistant` for a run seat's), and until a peer is reached the node's restore blocks MUST say later pins may be missing. The node's principal MAY choose, by a configuration setting under ADM-04, that the node reads its access token from an environment secret or a mounted file; `cairn status` MUST name where it reads the access token from, and Cairn MUST remove the access token from the environment of child processes. Minting an access token MUST be a widening principal act and revoking one (`cairn access-token revoke`) a cut one unless it removes a pin from a restore block, which makes it widening (OWN-11); its issue, use, rotation and revocation MUST be audited, and the issuing node MUST show an access token no writer used as `enrolled, never synced`. | T    | I6, I8  |
| PEER-07 | P2  | A peer MUST accept a relayed segment whose writer's seat key chains to a principal key in its key set (PRV-10), and MUST refuse events by a revoked key under PRV-10's rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | T    | I2, I8  |
| PEER-09 | P2  | Presence and typing hints MUST be ephemeral, never stored in the record, and attributed only to the peer's device key, or a token-key-only node's token key, that authenticated the connection. Drafts MUST stay private to their author unless the room's live-drafts setting allows them. The room view MUST show each connected seat's presence hint, and typing where the room's typing setting allows it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | T    | I2, I8  |
| PEER-10 | P2  | When one writer seals two different events at one `seq`, Cairn MUST keep both for forensics, mark the writer `equivocated`, and stop every derived artifact for that writer at the fork until the node's principal chooses which fork to keep, a widening principal act.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | T    | I6, I10 |
| PEER-11 | P2  | Every purge (ADM-07, ADM-14) MUST be sent as an erasure request, and every quarantine as a quarantine request, to every enrolled peer holding the room, as signed events, `operator`, or `structural` for an erasure request a retention policy sends. A peer's principal applies an erasure request by a widening principal act (OWN-11), and a quarantine request by one of the class of the quarantine it applies (SEC-12), refusing either by a neutral one, and a peer that applies one MUST run SEC-31 on every copy it holds in any writer. Each peer's state (applied, refused or unreachable) MUST be audited, counted and shown. A peer that refuses keeps its copy, and the room view MUST say so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | T    | I5, I6  |
| PEER-12 | P2  | A peer MAY be enrolled as a blind peer, for storage only. The node's principal MUST enroll a blind peer for a room only as the room's visibility allows (LANE-17). A blind peer MUST hold and serve a room's sealed ranges only encrypted to the device keys of the room's principals, or a token-key-only node's token key, never to seat keys, and MUST verify the seat key's signature over each encrypted range before storing it. It MUST learn nothing beyond writer ids, address ranges, sizes and signatures: no event content, payload, header field, commitment key or room metadata. It MUST NOT derive room state, have a seat in the room, or carry a principal act's authority. The room's principals MUST decrypt only on their own nodes. A change of the room's principals MUST encrypt every range sent afterwards to the device keys of the principals after the change; ranges a removed principal's nodes already received stay readable there, and the room view MUST say so. Every surface MUST mark a blind peer as one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | T    | I4, I8  |
| PEER-13 | P2  | A bar (LANE-25) MUST take effect on the node that records it as soon as it is recorded, and the peer component MUST stop sending the room's segments to every key the bar covers. A node MUST show every missing range in a writer it holds that can carry room acts beside the room's membership, never as a quiet end. A bar MUST stop only future content, and the room view MUST say that a barred seat keeps what its node already holds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | T    | I6, I8  |
