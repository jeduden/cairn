# 5. Functional requirements: room view

The room-view family of §5, kept in its own file. Each table lists:
**ID** · **Pri** · **Requirement** · **Ver** (verification method) ·
**Traces** (invariants).

## 5.12 Room view (VIEW)

VIEW holds what a person reads, on every surface: the browser room
view (the room-view component, B1), the TUI, the CLI and the paired
phone. Its surfaces are Fleet, Catch up, Needs you, Health, Setup and
Peers, and each room shows its outcome window. VIEW has no write path
of its own: it writes only through LANE-24's room acts and OWN's
principal acts. The priorities of rows that open the room view to v1
are provisional until OQ-30 settles the v1 scope.
