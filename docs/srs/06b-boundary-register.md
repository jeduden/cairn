# 6. Security: boundary register

## 6.3 Boundary register

Every Cairn component, process and protocol, with its boundary or why it is
rejected (SEC-19). The set is closed, as the
[domain model](../domain-model/components-and-surfaces.md) lists it. No
packaging (OQ-32) may change a row. "Disable" names the SEC-22 power that
covers the row: "yes" where managed policy can disable the row, else the
boundary or the launcher whose disabling covers it.

A Cairn feature whose purpose is to reach off the machine through a
carrier the principal runs MUST have its own row, be off by default and
be turned on by a widening principal act (SEC-19); row 11 is one. An
"outside" row names what leaves through a carrier Cairn does not run.
Rows 8, 10, 20 and 21 rest on who signs and seals what their component
records (open: OQ-40); row 17 rests on a paired phone's keys and writer
(open: OQ-39).
